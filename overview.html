Congressional App Challenge Project

Screen time that
turns into fitness.

Privacy-first fitness app that uses on-device AI to count reps in real time. No video leaves your device. Gamified with XP, ranks, and streaks.

Try Live Demo GitHub

Our inspiration came from our own screen time reports—easily 6+ hours a day. We didn't want to cut out the screen. We wanted to use it to grow ourselves.

Then we learned we weren't alone. Over half of US teens spend 4+ hours daily on screens, and since 1990, adolescent steps have dropped by almost 30%.

Teens love games, but most fitness apps don't make staying active fun. So we built RepQuest: a simple, intuitive app that works on almost any device, turning screen time into fitness time.
